About

I studied electrical engineering in Ottawa and found my way into software, which is where I've stayed for the past decade — mostly building the backend systems behind products used by millions of people.

These days I work on distributed systems: notification platforms, social features, and the infrastructure that has to hold up when everyone shows up at once. I care about systems that are reliable, extensible, and easy to operate.

Right now I spend most of my time thinking about AI as a collaborator rather than a tool, and what it changes about how software gets designed, tested, and shipped.

Outside of work you'll find me busy with my little ones, building with AI on my own time purely for the fun of it, or trying out a new recipe. Always happy to chat — feel free to reach out.
